## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio as an engineering-textbook figure plate. Every project is shown as a drawn figure of its real pipeline, captioned like a textbook figure, and a student's highlighter marks what matters. Refuses the dark neon dev portfolio with gradient cards and stock photos.
OWN-WORLD: Cool paper ground, blue-black ink, one fluorescent highlighter; nothing else. One family, Archivo variable (expanded heavy for the monument, normal width for text). Square corners everywhere. Diagrams in 1.5px ink strokes, the model step filled with highlighter. State is shown by highlighting (a per-line highlighter fill swept in), never by glow. Raises: total palette commitment (from datamatics); every control labelled in words (from the tape deck); known vs pending links drawn differently (from the provenance ribbon); featured work first, supporting work collapses by priority (from the developer console); each project carries its provenance ticket of discipline, year and stack (from the bazaar). Skills use three identical layer bands; the ruler raise was dropped after review rather than added as decoration. One deliberate colour field closes the page: Contact is set on the highlighter, its rows divided by ink rules, and the ink footer answers it; everywhere else the highlighter is a mark or a state.
STORY: Who he is (name, graduate, three layers), then proof (work figures, internships), then capability (model, software, interface layers), then about, then contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Plain top bar: wordmark left, four links plus Résumé right. Across the upper middle, the name in huge expanded type moves left as a slow marquee through a 1:1.3 crop of the studio portrait (a square could not hold the band without covering the face, so the band crosses the jacket instead); letters turn paper-coloured where they cross it. Under the portrait: one 24px sentence on the three layers and one fact line. Bottom row: location left, highlighter "Download résumé" rectangle and "See the work" right.
